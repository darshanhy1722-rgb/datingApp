2. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create your account, and as the law otherwise allows (for example, to comply with legal obligations). You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect processing that happened before, and without it we can't provide Spark to you.

3. What other members see

  • They see your first name, age, photos, prompt answers, the optional details you add, your area and hometown, and roughly how far away you are.
  • They never see your email, date of birth, password or exact location.
  • Only the person you message can see your messages.
  • People you block, or who block you, can't see you at all.

4. Who we share data with

  • We do not sell your personal data or use it for third-party advertising.
  • Service providers who host and run Spark for us ([hosting provider]), under contracts that require them to protect it.
  • OpenStreetMap (Nominatim): when you tap "Use my current location", your coordinates are sent from your browser to OpenStreetMap to look up your area name.
  • Google Fonts: your browser loads the app's font from Google, which may receive your IP address.
  • Authorities: when required by law, a court order or a lawful request from a government agency, or to protect someone's safety.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your data within [30] days, except where the law requires us to keep some information longer (for example, reports and records needed to investigate abuse or respond to law enforcement, which we keep for up to [180 days / period required by law]).

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • get a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it (most of it you can edit directly in the app);
  • have it erased, by deleting your account;
  • have your grievances addressed by us;
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to.

To use these rights, email [privacy email]. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Security

We protect your data with measures such as password hashing, access controls and encrypted connections (HTTPS) in production. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach happens, we will inform the Data Protection Board and affected members as the law requires.

8. Children

Spark is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete the account. You can report a member who may be under 18 from their profile.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will ask you to review and accept the new version in the app.
